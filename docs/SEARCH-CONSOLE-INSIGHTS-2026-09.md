## 6. Actionable Priorities: What to Optimize & Add More Of

### Priority 1: High-Impression / Low-CTR Optimization (Immediate Quick Wins)

1. **`showback vs chargeback` (410 Impressions, 0 Clicks):**
   - **Diagnosis:** Two competing URLs existed (`/research/showback-vs-chargeback` and `/research/chargeback-vs-showback`), cannibalizing ranking and fragmenting signals.
   - **Fix:** Consolidate onto the primary high-intent URL `/research/showback-vs-chargeback`. Update the title and `<meta name="description">` to match search intent: `"Showback vs Chargeback for AI: Key Differences, Models & Rules"`. Add a direct comparison summary table in the first 50 words to win Google's Featured Snippet and AI Overview citation box.
2. **`azure-openai-vs-direct-cost` (1,747 Impressions, 8 Clicks, 138 AI Overviews Impressions):**
   - **Diagnosis:** 1,747 impressions with only 8 clicks (~0.46% CTR).
   - **Fix:** Update snippet to highlight concrete savings and decision rules (e.g., *"When PTU reserved capacity beats pay-as-you-go tokens by up to 82%, regional pricing markups, and break-even calculators"*).
3. **`finops for llm` (263 Impressions, 1 Click):**
   - **Fix:** Update `/research/finops-for-llm` metadata to clearly target "FinOps for LLM: Complete Implementation Guide & 4 Pillars".
